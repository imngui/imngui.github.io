My work sits where human-robot interaction, motion planning, and robot learning meet. Recently I presented CIPHER at WAFR 2026 and ERUPT at ICAT-EGVE 2026, and I am running a pre-registered user study comparing immersive and desktop interfaces for robot motion planning.

Research Interests

XR for Human-Robot Interaction

Immersive interfaces for programming, previewing, and supervising robots. Users reshape the robot's workspace and the planner responds in real time.

Learning from Human Feedback

Learning robot preferences from demonstrations and corrections, including environment edits that disambiguate what a person actually cares about.

Multi-Robot Motion Planning

Scalable planners that coordinate robot teams through workspace-level guidance, for geometric and kinodynamic robots.
